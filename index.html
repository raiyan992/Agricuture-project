For learning only. Sensors can tell you when soil is dry or a tank is low, so water and effort are used better.

💧

A probe in the soil reports how wet it is, so you irrigate only when needed.

Fertilizer Guide

Vermicompost

Organic

Improves soil structure and feeds plants slowly. Mix into soil before sowing.

Urea (Nitrogen)

Chemical

Boosts leaf growth. Apply in split doses; avoid before heavy rain. Wear gloves and a mask.

DAP (Phosphorus)

Chemical

Helps roots and early growth. Place near the seed row at sowing.

MOP (Potassium)

Chemical

Improves grain filling and disease resistance. Apply at sowing or early growth.
